# Telecommunications Business Act Amendment Bill 2206775

> Bill 2206775 repealed the Mobile Device Distribution Improvement Act while transferring necessary provisions into the Telecommunications Business Act to promote fair distribution competition and improve user welfare.

## Basic info
- Bill number: 2206775
- Bill ID: PRC_Y2H4M1M1J2J5C1D7E0L0Q5J3M3S3O8
- Legislature: 22nd National Assembly
- Proposer: Chair of the Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee
- Proposal date: 2024-12-20
- Committee: Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee
- Passage date: 2024-12-26
- Result: Passed as proposed
- Status: Promulgated
- Promulgation: Act No. 20677, 2025-01-21

## Key Facts
- Roll-call 1316 passed the bill as proposed: 255 in favor, 4 against, and 4 abstentions; 37 members were absent.
