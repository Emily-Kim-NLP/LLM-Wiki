# Space Development Promotion Act Amendment Bill 2206765

> Bill 2206765 amends South Korea’s Space Development Promotion Act to enable specified explosives production for space launch vehicles and support space-development expos.

## Key Facts
- Passed as originally proposed on 2024-12-26; vote totals were 262 in favor, 1 against, 12 abstentions, and 25 nonparticipants.
- The source marks the member roster complete.
