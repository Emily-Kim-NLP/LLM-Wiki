# Artificial Intelligence Basic Act Bill 2206772

> Bill 2206772 established a legal framework for artificial intelligence development, trust, safety, and governance in South Korea.

## Basic info
- Bill number: 2206772
- Bill ID: PRC_R2V4H1W1T2K5M1O6E4Q9T0V7Q9S0U0
- Proposer: Chair of the National Assembly Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee
- Proposed: 2024-12-20
- Plenary consideration and passage: 2024-12-26
- Passage result: Passed as proposed
- Status: Promulgated
- Promulgation: Act No. 20676, 2025-01-21
- Responsible committee: Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee

## Key Facts
- Roll-call 1317 passed the bill as proposed: 260 in favor, 1 against, and 3 abstentions; 36 members were absent.
