# Oh Saehee

> Oh Saehee is a Democratic Party of Korea proportional-representation member of the 22nd National Assembly and a small-business policy advocate.

## Basic Info
- Korean name: 오세희
- Hanja: 吳世姬
- English name: OH SAEHEE
- Date of birth: 1955-12-27
- Gender: Female
- Party at collection time and latest recorded party: Democratic Party of Korea
- Election type and district: Proportional representation
- Seniority: First term
- Member codes: BRU2690F (primary); 9771391 (secondary)
- Committee: Trade, Industry, Energy, SMEs and Startups Committee
- Official email: oshee0530@gmail.com
- Official phone: 02-784-0496
- Website: https://blog.naver.com/oshee0530
- Education: Master’s degree in fashion design planning, Hansung University Graduate School of Arts

## Key Facts
- Oh Saehee serves as a member of the 22nd National Assembly and the Trade, Industry, Energy, SMEs and Startups Committee.
- Oh Saehee previously served as president of the Korea Federation of Micro Enterprise.
