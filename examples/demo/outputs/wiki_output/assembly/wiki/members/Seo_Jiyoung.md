# Seo Jiyoung

> Seo Jiyoung is a first-term People Power Party legislator representing Dongnae-gu, Busan.

## Basic Info
- Korean name: 서지영
- Hanja: 徐知英
- Date of birth: 1975-02-18
- Gender: Female
- Party: People Power Party
- District: Dongnae-gu, Busan
- Legislative term: 22nd National Assembly; first term
- Primary member code: P7U7800Z
- Alternate member code: 9771378
- Official email: jee21dongnae@naver.com
- Official phone: 02-784-8231
- Official website: https://m.blog.naver.com/jee21dongnae

## Key Facts
- Seo Jiyoung serves on the Trade, Industry, Energy, SMEs and Startups Committee and the Special Committee on Budget and Accounts.
- Seo Jiyoung earned a bachelor’s degree in sociology from Ewha Womans University and a master’s degree in public policy from Yonsei University Graduate School of Public Administration.
