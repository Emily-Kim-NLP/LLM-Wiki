# Park Sungjoon

> Park Sungjoon is a Democratic Party of Korea member of the 22nd National Assembly representing Seoul Jung-gu–Seongdong-gu B.

## Basic info
- Member code/person ID: IL74008M
- Korean name: 박성준
- Chinese characters: 朴省俊
- Date of birth: 1969-04-23
- Gender: Male
- Party at collection / latest recorded party: Democratic Party of Korea
- District: Seoul Jung-gu–Seongdong-gu B
- Election type and seniority: District representative; two terms
- Current committee in source record: Foreign Affairs and Unification Committee
- Official email: pksjoffice@naver.com
- Official phone: 02-784-8430
- Official website: https://blog.naver.com/pksjoffice

## Key Facts
- Park Sungjoon earned a bachelor's degree in political science from Hankuk University of Foreign Studies, a master's degree from Chungnam National University, and a doctorate from Sungkyunkwan University.
- Park Sungjoon served as a KBS announcer and JTBC announcer-team chief.
- Park Sungjoon held prior posts on the Education, Operations, National Defense, Legislation and Judiciary, Strategy and Finance, and Political Affairs committees.
