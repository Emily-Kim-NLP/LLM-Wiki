> Lee Hackyoung is a Democratic Party of Korea member of the 22nd National Assembly representing Gunpo, Gyeonggi.

## Key Facts
- Lee Hackyoung served as a member of the 19th, 20th, 21st, and 22nd National Assemblies.
- Lee Hackyoung served as National Assembly Deputy Speaker during the 21st Assembly.
- Lee Hackyoung held leadership roles in the National Council of YMCAs of Korea and civil-society organizations.
