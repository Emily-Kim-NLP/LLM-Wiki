# Park Sanghyuk

> Park Sanghyuk is a Democratic Party of Korea member representing Gimpo-si B, Gyeonggi, and serving in the 22nd National Assembly.

## Basic Info
- Korean name: 박상혁
- Hanja: 朴商赫
- English name: PARK SANGHYUK
- Date of birth: 1973-03-20
- Gender: Male
- Party at collection time and latest recorded party: Democratic Party of Korea
- Election type and district: Constituency; Gimpo-si B, Gyeonggi
- Seniority: Second term
- Member code: HXT76735
- Committee: Public Administration and Security Committee
- Official email: gimpo_park@naver.com
- Official phone: 02-784-2417~9
- Education: Law degree, Hanyang University; master’s degree in international trade law, Erasmus University

## Key Facts
- Park Sanghyuk served in the 21st and 22nd National Assemblies as the member for Gimpo-si B.
- Park Sanghyuk previously served on the House Steering Committee and the Land, Infrastructure and Transport Committee.
