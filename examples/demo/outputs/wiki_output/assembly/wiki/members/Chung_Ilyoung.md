# Chung Ilyoung

> Chung Ilyoung is a Democratic Party of Korea member of the 22nd National Assembly representing Incheon Yeonsu-gu B.

## Basic info
- Member code/person ID: LT72093S
- Korean name: 정일영
- Chinese characters: 鄭日永
- Date of birth: 1957-08-14
- Gender: Male
- Party at collection / latest recorded party: Democratic Party of Korea
- District: Incheon Yeonsu-gu B
- Election type and seniority: District representative; two terms
- Current committee in source record: Land, Infrastructure and Transport Committee
- Official email: chung10park@naver.com
- Official phone: 02-784-6651~3
- Official website: https://blog.naver.com/chung10park

## Key Facts
- Chung Ilyoung served in the 21st and 22nd National Assemblies.
- Chung Ilyoung passed the 23rd Higher Civil Service Examination.
- Chung Ilyoung formerly served as president of Incheon International Airport Corporation and chair of the Korea Transportation Safety Authority.
