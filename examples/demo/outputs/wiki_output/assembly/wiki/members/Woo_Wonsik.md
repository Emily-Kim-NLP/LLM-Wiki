# Woo Wonsik

> Woo Wonsik is a Democratic Party of Korea legislator representing Seoul Nowon-gu A and served as Speaker of the 22nd National Assembly in its first half.

## Basic info
- Korean name: 우원식
- English name: WOO WONSHIK
- Hanja: 禹元植
- Member code: XBT9550Q
- Date of birth: 1957-09-18
- Party at collection time: Democratic Party of Korea
- District: Seoul Nowon-gu A
- Election type: Constituency
- Service: Five-term legislator
- Committee assignment in source record: Foreign Affairs and Unification Committee
- Email: reform1-1@hanmail.net
- Official website: https://blog.naver.com/reform111

## Key Facts
- Woo Wonsik served as Speaker of the 22nd National Assembly in its first half.
- The source lists prior National Assembly service in the 17th, 19th, 20th, and 21st Assemblies.
- Prior positions include Democratic Party floor leader, chair of the 21st National Assembly Budget and Accounts Special Committee, and chair of the National Assembly Climate Crisis–Green New Deal Research Group.
- The source lists Woo Wonsik as Chair in the speaker roster for the 2024-12-26 plenary sitting.
