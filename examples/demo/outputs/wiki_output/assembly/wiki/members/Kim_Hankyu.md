# Kim Hankyu

> Kim Hankyu is a second-term Democratic Party of Korea legislator representing Jeju-si B, Jeju Province.

## Basic Info
- Korean name: 김한규
- Hanja: 金翰奎
- Date of birth: 1974-07-01
- Gender: Male
- Party: Democratic Party of Korea
- District: Jeju-si B, Jeju Province
- Legislative service: 21st and 22nd National Assemblies
- Primary member code: NUB4941Z
- Official email: hanqkim74@gmail.com
- Official phone: 02-784-5621
- Official website: https://www.instagram.com/hanqkim74

## Key Facts
- Kim Hankyu serves on the National Assembly Steering Committee and the Legislation and Judiciary Committee.
- Kim Hankyu previously served as a political secretary at the Blue House under the Moon Jae-in administration.
- Kim Hankyu passed the 41st Korean Bar Examination, is a New York attorney, and holds a Master of Laws degree from Harvard Law School.
