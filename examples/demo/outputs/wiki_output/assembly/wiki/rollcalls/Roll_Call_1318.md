# Roll Call 1318: Nuclear Safety Act Amendment Bill Vote

> Roll call 1318 records the 2024-12-26 plenary vote on Bill 2206769, which passed as originally proposed.

## Key Facts
- Roll-call ID: 1318; associated bill: 2206769, the committee alternative to partially amend the Nuclear Safety Act.
- Bill site ID: `PRC_L2S4Q1U2M0A2J1I1S3N4H3B0Z5M9U8`.
- Vote date: 2024-12-26; result: passed as originally proposed.
- Of 300 members, 260 voted in favor, 0 against, 11 abstained, and 29 did not participate.
- The responsible committee was the Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee.
