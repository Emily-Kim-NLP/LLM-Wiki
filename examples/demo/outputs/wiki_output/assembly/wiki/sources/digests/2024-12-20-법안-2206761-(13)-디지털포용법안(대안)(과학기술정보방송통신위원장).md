# Bill 2206761: Digital Inclusion Act Alternative (1/3)

> Source: National Assembly bills.csv record 6785 | 2024-12-20

## Summary
Bill 2206761, the Digital Inclusion Act alternative proposed by the chair of the Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee, passed the plenary session on 2024-12-26 and is recorded as promulgated. The alternative passed the committee on 2024-11-26, received a modified approval in the Legislation and Judiciary Committee on 2024-12-17, and passed the plenary session as originally proposed. The Act was promulgated as Act No. 20672 on 2025-01-21. The alternative incorporated and discarded seven earlier bills. The plenary tally was 263 in favor, zero opposed, and zero abstaining.

- The plenary session approved the bill as originally proposed on 2024-12-26; 263 members voted in favor, none opposed, and none abstained.
- Government transfer occurred on 2025-01-10; promulgation was on 2025-01-21 as Act No. 20672, the Digital Inclusion Act.
- Seven earlier proposals were incorporated and discarded: bills 2204377, 2203139, 2202792, 2202129, 2201206, 2201165, and 2200551.
