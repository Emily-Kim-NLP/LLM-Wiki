# Bill 2206775: Telecommunications Business Act Amendment Bill (3/3)

## Summary
The bill’s stated purpose was to repeal the Mobile Device Distribution Improvement Act while transferring necessary provisions into the Telecommunications Business Act, encouraging fair competition in mobile-device distribution, and improving user welfare. The amendment also addresses business-closure orders, market oversight by the Korea Communications Commission, used-device transactions, emergency suspension orders, and data-submission requirements.

## Key Facts
- The bill proposed repealing the Mobile Device Distribution Improvement Act and transferring necessary definitions and provisions into the Telecommunications Business Act.
- The bill requires consideration of a justifiable reason before imposing sanctions, including business-closure orders, for failure to conduct business for a specified period.
- The bill provides for benefits such as service-fee discounts for users who subscribe without receiving a subsidy.
- The bill prohibits unfairly discriminatory subsidies based on users’ residential area, age, or physical condition.
- The bill provides for regulation of unfair manufacturer conduct and assigns market-management responsibilities to the Korea Communications Commission.
- The bill establishes a certification basis for trustworthy used-device sellers and provisions for emergency suspension orders and data submissions.
