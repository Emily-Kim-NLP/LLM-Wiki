# Roll Call 1317: Bill 2206772 Artificial Intelligence Basic Act

> Source: National Assembly roll-call record | 2024-12-26

## Summary
Roll-call 1317 records the 22nd National Assembly plenary vote on Bill 2206772, the alternative Artificial Intelligence Basic Act submitted by the chair of the Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee. The bill passed as proposed on 2024-12-26. Of 300 members, 260 voted in favor, 1 against, 3 abstained, and 36 were absent.
