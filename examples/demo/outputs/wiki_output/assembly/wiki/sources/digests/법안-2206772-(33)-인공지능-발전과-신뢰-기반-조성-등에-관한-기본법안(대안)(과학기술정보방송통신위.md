# Bill 2206772: Purpose and Main Provisions

## Summary
The alternative bill aims to support sound artificial intelligence development and establish a foundation of trust while protecting people’s rights and dignity, improving quality of life, and strengthening national competitiveness. Provisions cover national AI planning and governance, research and workforce development, ethical principles, safety and reliability, disclosure duties for AI-generated or high-impact AI services, and government oversight.

## Key Facts
- The bill defines artificial intelligence, high-impact artificial intelligence, generative artificial intelligence, AI ethics, and AI operators.
- The Minister of Science and ICT is to establish a national AI basic plan every three years, subject to deliberation and resolution by the National AI Committee.
- The bill provides for a presidential National AI Committee and permits designation of an AI Policy Center and operation of an AI Safety Research Institute.
- AI businesses must provide specified notices and labels for high-impact AI, generative AI, and synthetic outputs that may be difficult to distinguish from reality.
- AI businesses must take safety measures for qualifying AI systems and for high-impact AI products or services.
- The bill authorizes government investigation and corrective orders for violations.
