# Bill 2206769: Nuclear Safety Act Amendment Bill (Alternative), Part 1

## Summary
Bill 2206769 is an alternative amendment to the Nuclear Safety Act, proposed by the chair of the Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee on 2024-12-20. The bill passed as originally proposed on 2024-12-26 and was promulgated as Act No. 20721 on 2025-01-21. The record includes its legislative history, relationships to three superseded bills, and a plenary vote tally of 260 in favor, 0 against, and 11 abstentions.

## Key Facts
- Bill number: 2206769; bill ID: PRC_L2S4Q1U2M0A2J1I1S3N4H3B0Z5M9U8.
- Sponsor: Chair of the Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee.
- Proposed: 2024-12-20; passed: 2024-12-26; status: promulgated.
- Promulgation: Act No. 20721, Nuclear Safety Act, promulgated 2025-01-21.
- The plenary vote recorded 260 in favor, 0 against, and 11 abstentions, with 271 members present.
- Bills 2204408, 2203741, and 2202464 were incorporated into the alternative and discarded.
