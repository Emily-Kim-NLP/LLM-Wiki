# Bill 2206769: Nuclear Safety Act Amendment Bill (Alternative), Part 3

## Summary
The bill clarifies the legal process for continued operation of nuclear power reactors and related facilities after the design life expires. The bill also consolidates the statutory basis for nuclear safety management charges, reduces the maximum late-payment charge, permits collection deferrals in specified circumstances, and expands uses of the Nuclear Fund’s Nuclear Safety Regulation Account.

## Key Facts
- Continued operation after expiration of design life requires periodic safety reviews, submission of results to the Commission, and a change permit under Article 21(3).
- The bill consolidates the legal basis for nuclear safety management charges in the Nuclear Safety Act and revises the stated purpose of the charges.
- The maximum late-payment charge is reduced to 20% of the overdue charge; collection may be deferred when payment is prevented by natural disasters or similar circumstances.
- Permitted uses of the Nuclear Safety Regulation Account are expanded to include policy research and analysis, safety culture, Nuclear Safety Foundation operating costs, and account administration.
- The stated rationale identifies an existing lack of explicit statutory procedure for continued operation after reactor design-life expiry.
