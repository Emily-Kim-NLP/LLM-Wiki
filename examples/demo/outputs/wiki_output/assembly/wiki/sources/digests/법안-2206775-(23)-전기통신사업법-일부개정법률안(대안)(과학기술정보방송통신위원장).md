# Bill 2206775: Telecommunications Business Act Amendment Bill (2/3)

## Summary
This source paragraph supplies the member-level voting list for Bill 2206775’s plenary vote. The recorded tally was 255 votes in favor, 4 against, and 4 abstentions. The source identifies voting members by name, party, and member code. All four abstentions were members of the Democratic Party of Korea. The four votes against came from two members of the Progressive Party, one member of the Basic Income Party, and one member of the Democratic Party of Korea.
