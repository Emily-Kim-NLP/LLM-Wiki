# Roll Call 1316: Bill 2206775 Telecommunications Business Act Amendment

> Source: National Assembly roll-call record | 2024-12-26

## Summary
Roll-call 1316 records the 22nd National Assembly plenary vote on Bill 2206775, the alternative Telecommunications Business Act Amendment submitted by the chair of the Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee. The bill passed as proposed on 2024-12-26. Of 300 members, 255 voted in favor, 4 against, 4 abstained, and 37 were absent.
