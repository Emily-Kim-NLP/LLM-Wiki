# Bill 2206761: Digital Inclusion Act Alternative (2/3)

> Source: National Assembly bills.csv record 6785 | 2024-12-20

## Summary
The proposed Digital Inclusion Act aims to enable all members of society to benefit from intelligent information technology without discrimination or exclusion, improve quality of life and social integration, and support related industries and technology. The proposal defines digital inclusion, sets out duties concerning digital capability, alternative access methods, and prevention of adverse effects, and establishes recurring plans, impact assessments, capacity-building activities, and support for accessibility and inclusion technology. The source also supplies the names and party affiliations of all 263 affirmative voters in the plenary vote.

## Key Facts
- The bill defines digital inclusion as creating economic, social, and cultural conditions in which everyone can enjoy the benefits of intelligent information technology without discrimination or exclusion.
- The proposal calls for a basic digital-inclusion plan every three years and annual implementation plans.
- The proposal introduces digital-inclusion impact assessments to support integrated policy development and implementation.
- Measures include digital capability-building activities, designation of digital capability centers, accessibility quality certification, and promotion of priority purchasing for accessibility.
- Technology and service provisions include standardization, designation and support of promising technologies and services, research and development, dissemination, cost support, and export-promotion measures.
- The plenary vote recorded 263 affirmative votes, with no opposing or abstaining votes; the supplied member-level list identifies voters by name, party, and member code.
