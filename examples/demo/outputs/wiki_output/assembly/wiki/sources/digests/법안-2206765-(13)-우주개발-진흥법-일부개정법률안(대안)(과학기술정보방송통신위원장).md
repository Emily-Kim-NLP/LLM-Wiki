# Bill 2206765: Space Development Promotion Act Amendment (Alternative)

## Summary
Bill 2206765 is an alternative bill introduced by the chair of the Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee on 2024-12-20. The National Assembly passed the bill as originally proposed on 2024-12-26. The bill was promulgated as Act No. 20674 on 2025-01-21. The plenary roll call recorded 262 votes in favor, 1 against, and 12 abstentions, with 275 members present.

## Key Facts
- Bill number: 2206765; bill ID: PRC_Y2L4B1A2B0P2P1Q1F2K6E5G2E6O0K2.
- Introduced on 2024-12-20 by the chair of the Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee.
- Passed as originally proposed on 2024-12-26 and recorded as promulgated.
- Promulgation: Act No. 20674, dated 2025-01-21.
- Plenary vote: 262 in favor, 1 against, 12 abstentions; 275 members present out of 300.
- Bill 2206765 is an alternative consolidating two bills, numbers 2204594 and 2204072.
