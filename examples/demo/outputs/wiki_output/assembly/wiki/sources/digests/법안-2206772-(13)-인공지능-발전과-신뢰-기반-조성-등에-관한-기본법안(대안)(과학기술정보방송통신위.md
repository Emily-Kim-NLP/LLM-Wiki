# Bill 2206772: Artificial Intelligence Basic Act — Bill Record and Legislative History

## Summary
Bill 2206772, the alternative bill titled “Framework Act on the Development of Artificial Intelligence and the Establishment of a Foundation for Trust,” was proposed by the National Assembly Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee chair on 2024-12-20. The plenary approved the bill as proposed on 2024-12-26. The bill was promulgated as Act No. 20676 on 2025-01-21. The bill record documents committee and Judiciary Committee review, transmission to the government, a plenary vote tally, and the linked minutes record T52677.

## Key Facts
- Bill number: 2206772; bill ID: PRC_R2V4H1W1T2K5M1O6E4Q9T0V7Q9S0U0.
- Sponsor: Chair of the National Assembly Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee.
- Proposal date: 2024-12-20; plenary passage date: 2024-12-26; result: passed as proposed.
- Promulgated as Act No. 20676 on 2025-01-21.
- Vote tally: 260 in favor, 1 opposed, 3 abstentions; 264 members present out of 300.
- The linked plenary minutes identifier is 52677; the linked roll-call ID is 1317.
