# Bill 2206769: Nuclear Safety Act Amendment Bill (Alternative), Part 2

## Summary
The member-level vote record for Bill 2206769 lists 260 members voting in favor, 11 abstaining, and none voting against. The source gives each participating member’s name, party affiliation, and member code. The recorded result is consistent with the aggregate tally.

- No member voted against.
- Each listed member is identified by name, party, and unique member code.
- The abstentions included 7 members of the Democratic Party of Korea, 2 members of the Progressive Party, and 2 members of the Rebuilding Korea Party.
