# Bill 2206765: Space Development Promotion Act Amendment (Alternative), Rationale and Links

## Summary
The bill addresses barriers to supplying explosives for commercial space-launch vehicles and establishes authority for public-sector space-development expos. The source also confirms links to plenary roll-call 1319 and minutes record T52677.

## Key Facts
- Proposed new Article 18-8 permits the Commissioner General of the Korean National Police Agency to authorize production of space-launch vehicle explosives at military explosives manufacturing facilities, notwithstanding the explosives-manufacturing permit requirements specified in the relevant safety law.
- Proposed new Article 18-9 authorizes the national government or local governments to hold space-development expos or support event budgets.
- The rationale identifies separate-facility requirements under the Act on the Safety Management of Guns, Swords, and Explosives as a barrier to supplying civilian space-launch businesses.
- The rationale states that expos could support national image, company brand value, international exchange, civic awareness, and economic activity.
- The bill is linked to roll-call ID 1319 and minutes ID T52677.
