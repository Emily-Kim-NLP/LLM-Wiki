# Bill 2206775: Telecommunications Business Act Amendment Bill (1/3)

## Summary
Bill 2206775, a Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee alternative to amend the Telecommunications Business Act, was proposed on 2024-12-20 and passed by the plenary on 2024-12-26. The bill was promulgated as Act No. 20677 on 2025-01-21. The recorded vote was 255 in favor, 4 against, and 4 abstentions, with 263 members present.
