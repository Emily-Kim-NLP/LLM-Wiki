# Wiki Directory Overview


> **Knowledge Overview** (updated 2026-10-27)
>
> This knowledge base supports multi-hop question answering about South Korea’s 22nd National Assembly by linking structured records on bills, legislators, plenary roll-call votes, and meeting minutes. Its five bill records cover identifying details, sponsors, committees, proposal rationales and contents, legislative progress, passage, and promulgation. The proposals include the Artificial Intelligence Basic Act (Bill 2206772), establishing a framework for AI development, trust, safety, and governance; the Digital Inclusion Act (2206761), addressing digital capability, accessibility, and inclusion; the Telecommunications Business Act Amendment (2206775), transferring provisions from the repealed Mobile Device Distribution Improvement Act to promote fair competition and user welfare; the Nuclear Safety Act Amendment (2206769), concerning continued-operation authorization and nuclear safety financing; and the Space Development Promotion Act Amendment (2206765), addressing specified explosives for launch vehicles and support for space-development expos. Five roll-call records connect bills to plenary outcomes and, where available, individual members’ votes; the Digital Inclusion Act vote on 2024-12-26 is recorded as 263 in favor, with none opposed or abstaining. Two minutes records provide meeting context, including T52677, which links Bill 2206761 to the 420th Assembly’s first plenary meeting on 2024-12-26, and T52678, covering a plenary meeting held the following day. The 23 member profiles identify legislators by name and member code and describe party affiliation, constituency or proportional-representation status, service history, committee assignments, and biographies. They represent the People Power Party, Democratic Party of Korea, and Rebuilding Korea Party, enabling member-level questions that can connect a legislator to a vote, bill, party, or institutional role. Across these domains, bill numbers, member codes, roll-call IDs, and minute IDs support tracing proposals from rationale and committee process through plenary consideration and voting. The 90 source pages comprise paragraph digests and original archives that underpin the structured records. The collection reflects supplied records and information available at collection time; empty or incomplete fields should not be treated as evidence that information or records do not exist.


## Directory Catalog

- **bills/** (5 pages) — bills — Legislative proposals and bills, including their status, rationale, contents, and processing history.
- **members/** (23 pages) — members — Biographical and institutional profiles of elected legislators.
- **minutes/** (2 pages) — minutes — Official records of plenary or committee meetings, including agendas and proceedings.
- **rollcalls/** (5 pages) — rollcalls — Recorded votes on legislative items, including totals and member-level voting lists.
- **sources/** (90 pages) — Source pages — paragraph digests and original archives
- **syntheses/** (0 pages) — syntheses — Analytical or summary pages that combine information across multiple records.
