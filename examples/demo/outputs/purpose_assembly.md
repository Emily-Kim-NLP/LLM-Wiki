# Purpose

This knowledge base supports multi-hop question answering about South Korea’s National Assembly, using structured parliamentary records. The samples focus on the 22nd National Assembly and connect bills to plenary roll-call votes, individual members, and meeting minutes.

It contains information on:
- **Bills:** identifying details, sponsors, committees, dates, passage and promulgation status, legislative progress, and proposal rationales and summaries.
- **Members:** names and identifiers, party, district, election and service history, committee assignments, and biographies.
- **Roll-call votes:** outcomes and vote totals, with member-level vote lists where available.
- **Meeting minutes:** session metadata and agenda items.

Records are linked through identifiers such as bill numbers, member codes, roll-call IDs, and minute IDs, enabling questions that combine facts across these sources—for example, tracing a bill to its vote and meeting, or identifying how a particular member voted. The data represents the supplied records and collection-time information; empty or incomplete fields should not be treated as evidence that information does not exist.
